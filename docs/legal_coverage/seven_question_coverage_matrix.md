## Version and ingestion blockers

- [Acquisition manifest](acquired/manifest.json) records the original URL, resolved URL, acquisition time, byte length and hash for 17 archived official responses. The [acquisition validation](acquisition-validation.json) confirms file hashes and probes HTML structures and PDF page readability. Three archived responses are register or version locators. The Civil and Labour Code static HTML records contain normative text and are distinct from the locator pages.
- The consolidated No. 189 page contains superseded text and live provisions together. The active rules in §§1.5 and 4.2–4.4 need an applicability interval and amendment references before use in gold data. Promoting plain text scraped from the page could make struck-out content eligible for citation. [Official consolidation](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/3/f_3468.html)
- Cabinet No. 187 was adopted on 13 May 2022. Its original PDF and later amendments must be assembled by version. Its medical classification table does not supply payment percentages by itself. [Official register](https://e-qanun.az/framework/49623) · [Example Cabinet amendment](https://nk.gov.az/uploads/doc/docs/64bfc27821313.pdf)
- Cabinet No. 269 was adopted on 1 August 2014. Preserve the numbered injury list, its scope and cited amendments. Cabinet No. 27 was adopted on 28 February 2000 and has a separate commencement clause. Preserve the forms and their field relationships. [Injury list](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/28/f_28241.html) · [Investigation rules](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/0/f_232.html)
- Workplace Insurance Law No. 999-IIIQ was adopted on 11 May 2010. Reconcile the indexed PDF against the official amendment list and any later effective changes. The Civil Code was approved on 28 December 1999 by No. 779-IQ. The official static consolidation is archived and contains candidate provisions 919.1, 923.1 and 935.1. Confirm operative versions and extract the entire insurance chapter before indexing its provisions. The Labour Code was approved on 1 February 1999 by No. 618-IQ. Its archived consolidation contains arts. 74 and 217. [Presidential text](https://president.az/az/articles/view/69168/print)
- The No. 189 consolidation identifies Cabinet Decision No. 31 of 30 January 2026 and No. 307 of 21 September 2026. Obtain complete, readable copies of both amendment instruments and their exact commencement clauses. The register links alone cannot establish historical calculation rules. [No. 31](https://e-qanun.az/framework/61295) · [No. 307](https://e-qanun.az/framework/62632)

Archived HTML, effective intervals, active-clause extraction, table integrity, and cross-reference resolution must be validated before creating a new prepared index. An evidence table should preserve the heading, units, conditions, row labels and effective period with each row.
