The Civil Code and the Insurance Contract
=========================================

The Civil Code regulates the insurance contract as a specific type of obligation. Key elements:

- Parties: the insurer and the person concluding the contract (policyholder / insured). Contracts may also be concluded in favour of third parties.
- Subject: the insurer's obligation to make an insurance payment if an insured event occurs, in return for payment of the premium.
- Key concepts: insured event, sum insured, premium, insurance period, insurance payment.
- Insurable interest: there must be a lawful interest in the insured property or risk.
- Subrogation: after paying, the insurer may acquire a claim against the person who caused the loss (in cases and to the extent provided by law).
- Disclosure: the policyholder must correctly report material circumstances affecting the risk.

The Civil Code was aligned with amendments to the Law "On Insurance Activity" in 2019 (1637-VQD, 9 July 2019).
