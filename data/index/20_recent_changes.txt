Recent Changes and Practical Matters
====================================

Examples of key legal changes in recent years:
- 2018-2019: amendments to the Law "On Insurance Activity" (1400-VQD, 1618-VQD); alignment with the Civil Code (1637-VQD).
- 2019: the Law "On Agricultural Insurance".
- 2023: amendments to the Law "On Compulsory Insurance" by No. 949-VIQD of 6 July 2023, and related presidential acts.
- 2024: Central Bank Corporate Governance Standards for Insurers (21 February 2024, No. 09/2).
- Ongoing: expansion of compulsory medical insurance and electronic systems.

Practical advice:
1. Read the terms, exclusions and payment rules carefully before signing.
2. If an insured event occurs, give timely written notice and collect the required documents.
3. In a dispute, first file a written complaint with the insurer; if unresolved, you may apply to the Central Bank or a court.
4. Check an insurer's licence in the Central Bank's official list.
5. Use e-qanun.az and cbar.az for current versions of legal acts.
