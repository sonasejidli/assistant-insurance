Corporate Governance in Insurers
================================

By Central Bank Board Decision No. 09/2 of 21 February 2024, the "Corporate Governance Standards for Insurers" were approved. They rely on Articles 25.3 and 35.3 of the Law "On Insurance Activity".

Main directions (in general terms):
- functions and responsibility of governing bodies (supervisory board, executive body);
- risk management, internal control and internal audit;
- preventing conflicts of interest;
- transparency and disclosure (reporting) requirements;
- fit-and-proper criteria for senior persons.

The Standards aim to make an insurer's organisational structure professional and accountable. For article-by-article requirements, consult the original document on the Central Bank website.
