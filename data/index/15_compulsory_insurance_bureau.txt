The Compulsory Insurance Bureau
===============================

The Compulsory Insurance Bureau is one of the key institutions of the compulsory insurance system, performing functions set by the Law "On Compulsory Insurance".

Main functions:
- managing the unified electronic information system for compulsory insurance;
- representing Azerbaijan in the Green Card system;
- taking part in settlement and payment mechanisms (for example, payments to injured persons in cases the law provides, such as an insurer's insolvency or licence revocation);
- exchanging data with state registers and other information systems (immovable property register, Electronic Notary, etc.);
- coordination with market participants.

Insurers engaged in compulsory insurance must be members (participants) of the Bureau. The exact scope of powers and the funding mechanism are set in the current wording of the law.
