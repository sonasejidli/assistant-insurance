Agricultural Insurance
======================

The Law "On Agricultural Insurance" was adopted in 2019. Its aim is to protect agricultural producers from natural and other risks.

By Cabinet of Ministers resolution, the "Agricultural Insurance Rules" were approved. They:
- are governed by the Civil Code, the Law "On Insurance Activity" and the Law "On Agricultural Insurance";
- cover the agricultural insurance class (under Article 14.3.2.6-4 of the Law "On Insurance Activity");
- define the subject of insurance and the risks covered;
- do not apply to cases of declared quarantine: in such cases phytosanitary and veterinary measure costs are covered from the state budget under the laws "On Phytosanitary Control" and "On Veterinary Medicine".

The state may use mechanisms such as subsidising part of the premium to encourage agricultural insurance. For subsidy conditions, see current Cabinet of Ministers resolutions.
