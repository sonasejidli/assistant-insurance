The Law "On Insurance Activity"
===============================

The Law was adopted on 25 December 2007 as No. 519-IIIQ. Its purpose is to develop the insurance market, regulate professional market participants (insurers, reinsurers, intermediaries) and protect the rights of the insured.

Main topics covered:
- basic concepts and insurance types (life and non-life);
- requirements for insurers and licensing;
- charter capital, solvency and insurance reserves;
- activity of insurance intermediaries;
- reinsurance and co-insurance;
- state supervision and regulation.

The Law has been amended many times, for example by Law No. 1400-VQD of 18 December 2018 and by the 2019 introduction of the concept of "co-insurer" (1618-VQD, 27 June 2019).

The Central Bank adopts sub-legislative acts relying on various articles of the Law (for example 16.5, 25.3, 35.3, 78.2 and 81-6.6).
