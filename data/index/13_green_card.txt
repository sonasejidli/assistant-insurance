The Green Card System
=====================

The Green Card is an international insurance system for motor third-party liability. In Azerbaijan, the Compulsory Insurance Bureau performs related functions.

Legal points:
- a vehicle registered in another country entering Azerbaijan must present a Green Card (Article 63-1.2 of the Law "On Compulsory Insurance"). If not presented, a border insurance contract rule applies (covered in the Central Bank's premium calculation Rule).
- contracts are concluded between the Bureau, participating insurers and a bank; a special Central Bank Rule regulates these relations (Articles 34.1 and 52-1.3 of the Law).

Practical importance: Azerbaijani drivers travelling abroad can also secure their civil liability through the Green Card system. Check which countries belong to the system on the Bureau's website.
