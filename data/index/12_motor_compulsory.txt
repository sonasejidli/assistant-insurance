Compulsory Motor Third-Party Liability Insurance
================================================

This is one of the most widespread compulsory insurance types in Azerbaijan (under the Law "On Compulsory Insurance").

Essence: the owner of a motor vehicle must insure civil liability for harm that may be caused to the life, health and property of others.

Key points:
- the insurance certificate (policy) can be issued electronically;
- the existence of an insurance contract is checked through an electronic information system, and this check applies for technical inspection;
- standard and limit sums insured and premium calculation are set by Central Bank Requirements and Rules (Articles 9.3 and 57.1 of the Law);
- premiums may be calculated using tariff coefficients (for example vehicle type, driver experience).

After an insured event, the injured party applies to the insurer. Payment is made according to the extent of loss and the limits set by law. Check current amounts in the Central Bank's latest Requirements.
