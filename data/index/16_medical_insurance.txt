Medical Insurance Legislation
=============================

Medical insurance is regulated by the Law "On Medical Insurance" (1999), covering voluntary and compulsory medical insurance.

Compulsory medical insurance (CMI):
- the concept of a compulsory medical insurance fund was added to the law, creating an institutional framework;
- the State Agency for Compulsory Medical Insurance acts as the state body;
- the list and tariffs of services under CMI are set in the "Service Package for Compulsory Medical Insurance" and updated by Cabinet of Ministers resolutions;
- CMI rules rely on relevant articles of the Law (e.g. 15-23.2).

Voluntary medical insurance: offered by insurers under contract and may cover services beyond CMI.

As this area develops quickly, follow the latest changes to the service package, tariffs and rules in Cabinet of Ministers documents.
