Insurance Intermediaries
========================

Insurance intermediaries are professional market participants. Main groups:

1. Insurance agents: act on behalf of and on the instructions of the insurer. Separate rules govern agents' activity in compulsory insurance.
2. Insurance brokers: usually represent the policyholder's interests and mediate the conclusion of insurance contracts.
3. Independent experts: assess losses and provide other expert services.
4. Foreign insurance brokers: documents confirming the right to operate abroad must be legalised or apostilled, with the Azerbaijani translation notarised.

A Central Bank Rule lists the documents for registration of insurers and intermediaries and for the independent expert certificate (tax registration document, employment document, etc.), under Articles 54, 81-4.1, 81-4.2, 94-2.2, 94-2.3 and 95.3 of the Law.

Intermediaries must give the insured correct and complete information and avoid conflicts of interest.
