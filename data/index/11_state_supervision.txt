State Regulation and Supervision of the Insurance Market
========================================================

Supervision and regulation of the insurance market are carried out by the Central Bank of the Republic of Azerbaijan. It adopts normative acts under the Law "On the Central Bank" (e.g. Articles 22.1.17 and 51.4) and the insurance laws.

Main powers:
- issue, suspend and revoke licences;
- approve rules, standards and requirements;
- demand reports from insurers and carry out inspections;
- apply supervisory measures to insurers and intermediaries;
- consider appeals related to protecting the rights of the insured.

The Cabinet of Ministers and the President adopt acts in specific areas (e.g. agricultural insurance rules, application of compulsory insurance). In earlier periods some normative acts were approved by the Ministry of Finance; these powers later passed to the Central Bank.
