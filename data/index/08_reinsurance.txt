Reinsurance and Co-insurance
============================

Reinsurance: an insurer transfers part of its risks to another insurer (the reinsurer). The aim is risk spreading and financial stability.

Points in legislation:
- Reinsurers are professional participants of the insurance market.
- Reinsurance of risks with foreign insurers is regulated by a special Central Bank Rule (Articles 10.2, 10.6 and 78.2 of the Law), which provides for selecting foreign partners on criteria such as credit rating and solvency.
- The reinsurers' share is taken into account in insurance reserves under the reserve calculation rules.

Co-insurance: several insurers jointly accept one risk. In 2019 the concept of "co-insurer" was added to the Law: an insurer licensed to operate exclusively on a co-insurance basis (1618-VQD, 27 June 2019).
