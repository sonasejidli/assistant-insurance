Overview of Insurance Legislation in Azerbaijan
===============================================

Insurance relations in Azerbaijan rest on a multi-level body of legal acts.

1. Base act: the Civil Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan sets the general legal framework for the insurance contract.
2. Main special laws:
   - Law "On Insurance Activity" (25 December 2007, No. 519-IIIQ): regulates market participants and how they operate.
   - Law "On Compulsory Insurance" (24 June 2011, No. 165-IVQ): regulates compulsory insurance types.
   - Laws on social insurance, medical insurance, agricultural insurance and other sector-specific laws.
3. Sub-legislative acts: presidential decrees, Cabinet of Ministers resolutions, and Central Bank rules, standards and requirements.

The logic of the system: the Civil Code provides general contract law, special laws regulate the market and compulsory types, and the Central Bank supervises and issues detailed rules.
