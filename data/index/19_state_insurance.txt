State Compulsory Personal Insurance and Special Laws
====================================================

For some categories, Azerbaijan provides compulsory insurance at state expense:

1. The Law "On State Compulsory Personal Insurance of Military Servicemen" provides for insuring servicemen's life and health at state expense, to strengthen their social protection. It applies together with the Civil Code and the laws "On Insurance Activity" and "On Compulsory Insurance".

2. Other special laws provide insurance for certain professional groups (for example law-enforcement personnel, rescue forces). Insured events, amounts and payment rules differ in each law.

3. Media reported a draft law on compulsory insurance of pupils and students undergoing practical training in state bodies, and of volunteers. Verify the draft's current status from official sources.

Special laws act as specialised norms relative to general insurance legislation; where they conflict, the special law's requirements are taken into account.
