The Law "On Compulsory Insurance"
=================================

The Law was adopted on 24 June 2011 as No. 165-IVQ. Presidential Decree No. 500 of 15 September 2011 concerns its application.

Compulsory insurance means insurance that specified persons are legally required to take out.

The Law covers:
- compulsory insurance types and their scope;
- sums insured and tariffs, calculation of premiums;
- the insurance certificate (which may be issued electronically);
- the status and functions of the Compulsory Insurance Bureau;
- use of electronic information systems;
- liability for failing to hold compulsory insurance.

The Law has been amended in recent years, for example by Law No. 949-VIQD of 6 July 2023, with a related presidential order on its implementation.
