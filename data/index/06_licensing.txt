Licensing of Insurers and Requirements
======================================

To carry out insurance activity, an insurer must hold a licence. Licences are issued by insurance classes and types.

Main requirements:
- legal-entity status and the organisational-legal form required by law;
- minimum charter capital;
- suitability (qualification and reputation) requirements for managers and shareholders;
- insurance reserves and solvency;
- actuarial function and internal control system;
- compliance with corporate governance standards.

Additional requirements exist for compulsory types: the Central Bank's "Additional Requirements" apply to granting permission to carry out compulsory insurance types (Article 4.5 of the Law "On Compulsory Insurance").

Breaches of licence requirements may lead the Central Bank to warnings, restrictions or revocation of the licence.
