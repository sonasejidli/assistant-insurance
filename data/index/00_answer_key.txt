ANSWER KEY - INSURANCE LAW OF AZERBAIJAN
========================================

01_general_overview: Overview of Insurance Legislation in Azerbaijan
  1. The Civil Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan.
  2. 25 December 2007, No. 519-IIIQ.
  3. 24 June 2011, No. 165-IVQ.
  4. Presidential decrees, Cabinet of Ministers resolutions, and Central Bank rules/standards/requirements.
  5. It supervises the market and issues detailed rules.

02_civil_code: The Civil Code and the Insurance Contract
  1. The insurer and the policyholder (insured).
  2. To make an insurance payment if an insured event occurs.
  3. The insurance premium.
  4. The insurer's right, after paying, to claim against the person who caused the loss.
  5. 1637-VQD of 9 July 2019.

03_law_on_insurance_activity: The Law "On Insurance Activity"
  1. 25 December 2007, No. 519-IIIQ.
  2. Any two of: insurers, reinsurers, insurance intermediaries.
  3. Life and non-life insurance.
  4. 1618-VQD of 27 June 2019.
  5. The Central Bank.

04_law_on_compulsory_insurance: The Law "On Compulsory Insurance"
  1. Insurance that specified persons are legally required to take out.
  2. Decree No. 500 of 15 September 2011.
  3. It may be issued electronically.
  4. The Compulsory Insurance Bureau.
  5. No. 949-VIQD of 6 July 2023.

05_insurance_types: Insurance Types and Classification
  1. Life insurance and non-life insurance.
  2. Compulsory vs voluntary; personal, property and liability insurance.
  3. Article 14.3.2.6-4 of the Law "On Insurance Activity".
  4. Insurance of a borrower's life against death and loss of working capacity.
  5. Only if it holds a licence for that type.

06_licensing: Licensing of Insurers and Requirements
  1. By insurance classes and types.
  2. Any two, e.g. minimum charter capital, suitability of managers, solvency and reserves, actuarial function.
  3. Article 4.5.
  4. The Central Bank.
  5. Managers and shareholders.

07_intermediaries: Insurance Intermediaries
  1. The insurer's.
  2. The policyholder's.
  3. Assess losses and provide other expert services.
  4. Legalisation or apostille, with a notarised Azerbaijani translation.
  5. Give correct and complete information; avoid conflicts of interest.

08_reinsurance: Reinsurance and Co-insurance
  1. An insurer transferring part of its risks to another insurer.
  2. Risk spreading and financial stability.
  3. 10.2, 10.6 and 78.2 of the Law.
  4. Several insurers jointly accepting one risk.
  5. An insurer licensed to operate exclusively on a co-insurance basis.

09_reserves_actuary: Insurance Reserves, Solvency and the Actuary
  1. To meet their obligations.
  2. Zero.
  3. Article 81-6.6.
  4. Reserves and tariffs.
  5. To protect the insured and beneficiaries from insolvency risk.

10_corporate_governance: Corporate Governance in Insurers
  1. Central Bank Board Decision No. 09/2 of 21 February 2024.
  2. 25.3 and 35.3.
  3. Any two, e.g. governing bodies, risk management, internal audit, conflicts of interest, disclosure.
  4. A professional and accountable organisational structure.
  5. On the Central Bank website.

11_state_supervision: State Regulation and Supervision of the Insurance Market
  1. The Central Bank of the Republic of Azerbaijan.
  2. Any two, e.g. issuing/revoking licences, approving rules, inspections, supervisory measures.
  3. The Cabinet of Ministers and the President.
  4. The Ministry of Finance.
  5. Reports (and it may carry out inspections).

12_motor_compulsory: Compulsory Motor Third-Party Liability Insurance
  1. Civil liability for harm to the life, health and property of others.
  2. Electronically.
  3. For technical inspection (and through the electronic information system generally).
  4. 9.3 and 57.1 of the Law.
  5. The injured party.

13_green_card: The Green Card System
  1. An international insurance system for motor third-party liability.
  2. The Compulsory Insurance Bureau.
  3. Article 63-1.2.
  4. The Bureau, participating insurers and a bank.
  5. On the Bureau's website.

14_real_estate: Compulsory Insurance of Immovable Property
  1. The Law "On Compulsory Insurance".
  2. Data on immovable property, in real time.
  3. "Electronic Notary".
  4. Any two, e.g. fire, explosion, natural events.
  5. Article 16.4.

15_compulsory_insurance_bureau: The Compulsory Insurance Bureau
  1. The unified electronic information system for compulsory insurance.
  2. The Green Card system.
  3. An insurer's insolvency or licence revocation (where the law provides).
  4. The immovable property register and the Electronic Notary.
  5. Insurers engaged in compulsory insurance.

16_medical_insurance: Medical Insurance Legislation
  1. The Law "On Medical Insurance" (1999).
  2. The compulsory medical insurance fund.
  3. The State Agency for Compulsory Medical Insurance.
  4. The Service Package for Compulsory Medical Insurance, updated by Cabinet resolutions.
  5. It is offered by insurers under contract and may cover services beyond CMI.
