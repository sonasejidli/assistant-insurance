Compulsory Insurance of Immovable Property
==========================================

Compulsory insurance of immovable property is one of the types regulated by the Law "On Compulsory Insurance". Under legislation, insurance of property owners' civil liability (for example harm to health and property) is also carried out on a compulsory basis.

Key points:
- special Central Bank Requirements cover the form and content of the compulsory insurance certificate for immovable property;
- data from the state register of immovable property is transmitted in real time to the Compulsory Insurance Bureau;
- data on notarial transactions is also transmitted to the Bureau from the "Electronic Notary" system;
- the subject and risks (e.g. fire, explosion, natural events) are set in the law and Central Bank acts.

Separate legal provisions exist for determining losses to state and municipal property (linked to Article 16.4 of the Law). Check sums insured and tariffs in current acts.
