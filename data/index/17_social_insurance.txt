Social Insurance Legislation
============================

Social insurance is regulated by the Law "On Social Insurance" (1997). It governs relations in social insurance and sets legal, economic and organisational foundations.

Key concepts:
- insurer: the body carrying out compulsory state social insurance;
- policyholders (insurance payers): employers, state bodies, and persons insuring themselves or others;
- insured persons: those for whose benefit insurance is carried out;
- insurance record: total periods of work for which compulsory state social insurance premiums were paid for the insured person.

Social insurance legislation consists of the Law and other normative acts. In the Alat Free Economic Zone, relations are governed by the requirements of the special law on that zone.

Related area: unemployment insurance, governed by a separate fund and rules. Exact conditions for pensions and benefits are set by special laws and current tariff schedules.
