Insurance Reserves, Solvency and the Actuary
============================================

An insurer must have enough financial resources to meet its obligations.

1. Insurance reserves: rules exist for creating and calculating reserves for life and non-life insurance. Reserves for compulsory types follow the compulsory insurance laws. A separate rule covers assets backing reserves (Article 81.8 of the Law).
2. Solvency and risk capital: Central Bank Rules set how risk capital is calculated; if the result is negative, risk capital is taken as zero.
3. Actuary: the Rule on content, form and submission of the actuarial opinion was approved under Article 81-6.6 of the Law. The actuary takes part in justifying reserves and tariffs.
4. Investment operations: a Rule governs insurers' investment operations and sets quality requirements for assets backing reserves.

The purpose is to protect the insured and beneficiaries from insolvency risk.
