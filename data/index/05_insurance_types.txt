Insurance Types and Classification
==================================

Legislation divides insurance into two main groups:

1. Life insurance: risks tied to a person's life, health and capacity to work (savings, risk cover, etc.).
2. Non-life insurance: property insurance, civil liability, accident, medical expenses, cargo, transport, financial risks, etc.

Other classifications:
- compulsory and voluntary insurance;
- personal, property and liability insurance;
- insurance classes: Article 14.3.2.6-4 of the Law "On Insurance Activity" defines the agricultural insurance class separately.

Practical example: insurance of a borrower's life against death and loss of working capacity under loan contracts is governed by a special Central Bank Rule (under Article 16.5 of the Law).

An insurer may operate in a given type only if it holds a licence for that type.
