# Temporal applicability and conflict hardening

The changes apply only to the existing opt-in source authority layer. The date schema and authority catalog were extended in place. Retrieval, evidence assembly ranking, the hardened verifier, production defaults, and Qdrant were preserved.

## Dates and provenance

The existing CaseDates object now accepts claim_notification_date and claim_date, where claim_date means filing or submission. Both are separate from the insured event, contract start and termination, and requested legal reference date. Missing dates remain null. Each asserted value has a provenance record with its input field, origin request_assertion and verified false. A reference date supplied through filters.as_of retains that origin. Contract-period comparison still uses the insured event. Filing after termination, filing on the same day as the event, and either missing claim date do not change the event-date relationship. No conclusion about claim timeliness is generated by this layer.

The catalog records the date type, value, exact passage reference, verification status and an explicit-commencement-clause provenance record anchored to the indexed text hash. The date is attached to the clause declaring it, with the target and exceptions separately recorded. The catalog does not distribute the date to unreviewed provisions or infer a current effective interval from a commencement date alone.

## Conflict records

The detector first checks cataloged issue, rule family and personal and insurance scopes. For scope-comparable evidence, closed effective and repeal intervals can establish disjoint dates. Independently documented rule periods can establish a transition, and overlapping periods are reported as potential temporal overlap. Missing or inconsistent bounds leave the temporal relationship unknown. A shared boundary date leaves the time relationship uncertain. Publication and amendment dates never stand in for effectiveness.

Both exact references and both provision records remain in each comparison. Temporal basis and relationship are distinct from potential substantive conflict. Scope uncertainty stays explicit. The detector never picks a winning provision. Verified disjoint periods remain visible as a disjoint comparison and are not counted as potential simultaneous conflicts.

Each missing_information entry contains a field, a zero-based reference_index or null for case-level facts, and a reason. C06 names the missing effective date for reference 0 and the missing repeal dates for references 0 and 1. The supplied effective date for reference 1 is preserved. Known inconsistent dates go in conflicting_information, separately from missing inputs. The expert-review flag remains explicit and resolution stays null. Unanchored scope, case chronology, and transition boundaries are reported when relevant.
